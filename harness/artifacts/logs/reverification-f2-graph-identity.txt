  What a human or a re-provisioning must do: replace the graph and the
  environment record ATOMICALLY, so that one provisioning act writes both the
  bytes and the record of account that describes them, and then let a gate
  measure their agreement from empty artifact trees. Nothing available inside
  this run reaches that, and nothing in this run may fabricate agreement by
  moving either side.
